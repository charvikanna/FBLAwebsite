Lesson 3: Budgeting & Saving

50/30/20 rule and emergency funds.

1 / 4: Content

The 50/30/20 Rule

50% Needs (rent, groceries, utilities), 30% Wants (dining, entertainment), 20% Savings.

Emergency Fund

Save 3–6 months of expenses in a high-yield savings account.
