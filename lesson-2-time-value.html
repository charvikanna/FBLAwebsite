Lesson 2: Time Value of Money (TVM)

PV, FV, annuities, and discount rates.

1 / 4: Content

Core Concept

Money today is worth more than the same amount tomorrow. FV = PV × (1 + r)^n

Present Value & Discounting

PV = FV / (1 + r)^n. NPV = sum of discounted cash flows minus initial investment. IRR = rate that makes NPV = 0.
