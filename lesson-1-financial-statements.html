Lesson 1: Introduction to Financial Statements

Understanding the three core financial statements.

1 / 4: Content

1. Income Statement

The Income Statement (P&L) shows a company's financial performance over a period. It flows: Revenue → COGS → Gross Profit → Operating Expenses → Net Income.

2. Balance Sheet

Snapshot at a point in time. Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Assets = what the company owns; Liabilities = what it owes; Equity = owners' stake.

3. Cash Flow Statement

How cash moves: Operating (day-to-day), Investing (CapEx, acquisitions), Financing (debt, equity, dividends).
